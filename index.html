How to box a tooth

Box the visible crown tightly, edge to edge, including partly erupted teeth. Leave out gum, lips and the mirror. Box a tooth only if you can tell which tooth it is; otherwise mark it uncertain with U. In mirror photos, number teeth by their real FDI position, as in the sheet.
